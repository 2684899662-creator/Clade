# 冰淇淋灵 · sp_food_ice_cream

## LV1

【LV1 淇淋灵】核心元素：冰淇淋、冰淇淋球、甜筒、糖粒、雪花。单一元素：矮宽软圆蛋，壳纹：横向腰带，蛋壳正面印有「冰淇淋」剪影，左肩破口露出糖粒（单一元素）。
画风：赛尔号式粗描边赛璐璐：粗黑描边 3~5px，大色块，主色 ≤ 3 色（#ffb8d8 / #8a5a2a / #fff8fa），两阶硬阴影，无渐变，高饱和，头大身小、造型夸张；Q 版精灵，不拟人；透明背景，512×512 起；脚下不画光圈 / 地圈 / 法阵；翅膀 / 光环 / 皇冠不默认添加
禁止：拟人化、脚下光圈、脱离名称、与同类 / 跨类雷同、套模板

[LV1 Ice Cream Mote] Core elements: ice cream, scoops, cone, sprinkles, snowflake. a single element: Squat soft egg, shell pattern: Horizontal bands, a Ice Cream silhouette stamped on the front, the left crack reveals sprinkles (a single element).
Style: Seer-style thick-outline cel: bold black outlines 3–5px, big flat colour blocks, at most 3 main colours (#ffb8d8 / #8a5a2a / #fff8fa), two-step hard shadows, no gradients, saturated, big head and small body, exaggerated; chibi spirit, never humanoid; transparent background, 512×512 or larger; no ground ring, floor glow or magic circle; no wings, halo or crown unless they are core elements
Avoid: anything humanoid, ground rings, anything unrelated to the name, looking like other spirits, templated designs
