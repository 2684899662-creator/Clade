# 蜻蜓灵 · sp_insect_dragonfly

## LV1

【LV1 蜓点灵】核心元素：蜻蜓、透明翅、荷叶、水滴、复眼。单一元素：上宽下尖蛋，壳纹：冰裂纹，蛋壳正面印有「蜻蜓」剪影，顶部破口露出水滴（单一元素）。
画风：赛尔号式粗描边赛璐璐：粗黑描边 3~5px，大色块，主色 ≤ 3 色（#3ab8d8 / #8af85a / #efffff），两阶硬阴影，无渐变，高饱和，头大身小、造型夸张；Q 版精灵，不拟人；透明背景，512×512 起；脚下不画光圈 / 地圈 / 法阵；翅膀 / 光环 / 皇冠不默认添加
禁止：拟人化、脚下光圈、脱离名称、与同类 / 跨类雷同、套模板

[LV1 Dragonfly Mote] Core elements: dragonfly, wings, lotus leaf, drop, compound eye. a single element: Top-heavy pointed egg, shell pattern: Crackle, a Dragonfly silhouette stamped on the front, the top crack reveals drop (a single element).
Style: Seer-style thick-outline cel: bold black outlines 3–5px, big flat colour blocks, at most 3 main colours (#3ab8d8 / #8af85a / #efffff), two-step hard shadows, no gradients, saturated, big head and small body, exaggerated; chibi spirit, never humanoid; transparent background, 512×512 or larger; no ground ring, floor glow or magic circle; no wings, halo or crown unless they are core elements
