# 面具灵 · sp_obj_mask

## LV1

【LV1 面纹灵】核心元素：面具、缎带、流苏、亮片、宝石。单一元素：S 曲斜长蛋，壳纹：鳞片纹，蛋壳正面印有「面具」剪影，左肩破口露出亮片（单一元素）。
画风：赛尔号式粗描边赛璐璐：粗黑描边 3~5px，大色块，主色 ≤ 3 色（#7a3bd8 / #f2c14e / #fff0f6），两阶硬阴影，无渐变，高饱和，头大身小、造型夸张；Q 版精灵，不拟人；透明背景，512×512 起；脚下不画光圈 / 地圈 / 法阵；翅膀 / 光环 / 皇冠不默认添加
禁止：拟人化、脚下光圈、脱离名称、与同类 / 跨类雷同、套模板

[LV1 Mask Mote] Core elements: mask, ribbon, tassel, sequin, gem. a single element: Leaning S egg, shell pattern: Scales, a Mask silhouette stamped on the front, the left crack reveals sequin (a single element).
Style: Seer-style thick-outline cel: bold black outlines 3–5px, big flat colour blocks, at most 3 main colours (#7a3bd8 / #f2c14e / #fff0f6), two-step hard shadows, no gradients, saturated, big head and small body, exaggerated; chibi spirit, never humanoid; transparent background, 512×512 or larger; no ground ring, floor glow or magic circle; no wings, halo or crown unless they are core elements
Avoid: anything humanoid, ground rings, anything unrelated to the name, looking like other spirits, templated designs
