# 芯片灵 · sp_mech_chip

## LV1

【LV1 芯点灵】核心元素：芯片、数据块、信号格、像素、逻辑门。单一元素：双层阶梯蛋，壳纹：刻度环，蛋壳正面印有「芯片」剪影，右肩破口露出像素（单一元素）。
画风：赛尔号式粗描边赛璐璐：粗黑描边 3~5px，大色块，主色 ≤ 3 色（#3a4a6a / #3ae8a8 / #eafff8），两阶硬阴影，无渐变，高饱和，头大身小、造型夸张；Q 版精灵，不拟人；透明背景，512×512 起；脚下不画光圈 / 地圈 / 法阵；翅膀 / 光环 / 皇冠不默认添加
禁止：拟人化、脚下光圈、脱离名称、与同类 / 跨类雷同、套模板

[LV1 Chip Mote] Core elements: chip, data, signal bars, pixel, logic gate. a single element: Two-tier stepped egg, shell pattern: Tick rings, a Chip silhouette stamped on the front, the right crack reveals pixel (a single element).
Style: Seer-style thick-outline cel: bold black outlines 3–5px, big flat colour blocks, at most 3 main colours (#3a4a6a / #3ae8a8 / #eafff8), two-step hard shadows, no gradients, saturated, big head and small body, exaggerated; chibi spirit, never humanoid; transparent background, 512×512 or larger; no ground ring, floor glow or magic circle; no wings, halo or crown unless they are core elements
Avoid: anything humanoid, ground rings, anything unrelated to the name, looking like other spirits, templated designs
