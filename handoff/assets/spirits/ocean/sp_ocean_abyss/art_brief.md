## LV1

【LV1 深点灵】核心元素：深海、深海光、暗流、气泡、诱光核。单一元素：竖柱长蛋，壳纹：刻度环，蛋壳正面印有「深海」剪影，右肩破口露出气泡（单一元素）。

禁止：拟人化、脚下光圈、脱离名称、与同类 / 跨类雷同、套模板

[LV1 Abyss Mote] Core elements: abyss, deep glow, undercurrent, bubble, lure core. a single element: Column egg, shell pattern: Tick rings, a Abyss silhouette stamped on the front, the right crack reveals bubble (a single element).
Style: Seer-style thick-outline cel: bold black outlines 3–5px, big flat colour blocks, at most 3 main colours (#1a2a5a / #5af8f8 / #d8e8ff), two-step hard shadows, no gradients, saturated, big head and small body, exaggerated; chibi spirit, never humanoid; transparent background, 512×512 or larger; no ground ring, floor glow or magic circle; no wings, halo or crown unless they are core elements
