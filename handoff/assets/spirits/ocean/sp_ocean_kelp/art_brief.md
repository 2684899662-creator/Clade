# 海藻灵 · sp_ocean_kelp

## LV1

【LV1 藻点灵】核心元素：海藻、海藻须、气泡、贝壳、水滴。单一元素：竖柱长蛋，壳纹：竖向接缝，蛋壳正面印有「海藻」剪影，顶部破口露出贝壳（单一元素）。
画风：赛尔号式粗描边赛璐璐：粗黑描边 3~5px，大色块，主色 ≤ 3 色（#3a9a5a / #8fe8d8 / #eafff4），两阶硬阴影，无渐变，高饱和，头大身小、造型夸张；Q 版精灵，不拟人；透明背景，512×512 起；脚下不画光圈 / 地圈 / 法阵；翅膀 / 光环 / 皇冠不默认添加
禁止：拟人化、脚下光圈、脱离名称、与同类 / 跨类雷同、套模板

[LV1 Kelp Mote] Core elements: kelp, kelp fronds, bubbles, shell, drop. a single element: Column egg, shell pattern: Vertical seams, a Kelp silhouette stamped on the front, the top crack reveals shell (a single element).
Style: Seer-style thick-outline cel: bold black outlines 3–5px, big flat colour blocks, at most 3 main colours (#3a9a5a / #8fe8d8 / #eafff4), two-step hard shadows, no gradients, saturated, big head and small body, exaggerated; chibi spirit, never humanoid; transparent background, 512×512 or larger; no ground ring, floor glow or magic circle; no wings, halo or crown unless they are core elements
Avoid: anything humanoid, ground rings, anything unrelated to the name, looking like other spirits, templated designs
