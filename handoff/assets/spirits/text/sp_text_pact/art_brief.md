## LV1

【LV1 契点灵】核心元素：契约、契约卷、链环、印纹、小盾。单一元素：圆角方蛋，壳纹：星点，蛋壳正面印有「契约」剪影，右肩破口露出印纹（单一元素）。
画风：赛尔号式粗描边赛璐璐：粗黑描边 3~5px，大色块，主色 ≤ 3 色（#8a3a2a / #e8c06a / #fff4e4），两阶硬阴影，无渐变，高饱和，头大身小、造型夸张；Q 版精灵，不拟人；透明背景，512×512 起；脚下不画光圈 / 地圈 / 法阵；翅膀 / 光环 / 皇冠不默认添加
禁止：拟人化、脚下光圈、脱离名称、与同类 / 跨类雷同、套模板

[LV1 Pact Mote] Core elements: pact, pact scroll, chain, seal, shield. a single element: Rounded-square egg, shell pattern: Speckles, a Pact silhouette stamped on the front, the right crack reveals seal (a single element).
Style: Seer-style thick-outline cel: bold black outlines 3–5px, big flat colour blocks, at most 3 main colours (#8a3a2a / #e8c06a / #fff4e4), two-step hard shadows, no gradients, saturated, big head and small body, exaggerated; chibi spirit, never humanoid; transparent background, 512×512 or larger; no ground ring, floor glow or magic circle; no wings, halo or crown unless they are core elements
